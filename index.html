Copy recordings by USB

Get your MicMemo Pro recordings onto a computer with the watch's charging cable. Your files stay on your computer: this page reads them right here and uploads nothing.

  1. Close MicMemo Pro on the watch and connect the watch to your computer.
  2. Open the watch's storage. On Windows it appears in File Explorer. On a Mac, you need a tool such as OpenMTP.
  3. Copy the folder GARMIN/Apps/DATA to your computer.
  4. Choose that copied folder below.

If you choose files, pick both the .DAT and .IDX files with the same name. MicMemo Pro's file is usually the largest one.
