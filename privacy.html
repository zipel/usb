MicMemo Pro privacy notice

Last updated November 7, 2026.

On the watch

Your recordings stay in the app's storage on your watch. The app doesn't use the internet, and it has no account and no analytics. We never receive your recordings or anything else about you.

Location

Save location is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the app saves where each recording was made, with the recording on your watch. Nothing is sent anywhere.

When you copy recordings by USB

The USB page runs entirely in your browser. Your files aren't uploaded anywhere. If a recording has a location, the page shows a link to OpenStreetMap. OpenStreetMap only receives those coordinates if you open the link.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations vary. It's your responsibility to follow the rules where you are, including getting consent where that's required.
